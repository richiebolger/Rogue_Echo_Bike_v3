Echo Bike
Tracker

The workout tracking app the Rogue Echo Bike v3 deserves. Live metrics, training zones, personal bests and structured workouts — via Bluetooth.

Download for Mac View on GitHub
⚡

Live Metrics

Power (W), W/kg, Cadence, Speed, Heart Rate, Calories, Distance — all updating in real time from your bike.

📊

Training Zones

Power zones Z1–Z7 (% FTP) and HR zones via Heart Rate Reserve (Karvonen method), live on each metric card.

🏋

Workout Builder

Build interval sessions from segments. 14 built-in templates including Tabata, FTP Test, Ramp Test, and VO2 Max intervals.

🏆

Personal Bests

Tracks max power, best avg power, best W/kg, max HR, and lifetime distance/calories/time per rider.

🧬

VO2 Max Estimates

Power-based (Hawley & Noakes via FTP) and HR-based (Uth formula) estimates after every session.

💾

Full Data Export

Per-session CSV + JSON, cumulative history CSV. All data stays on your Mac — no accounts, no cloud.
