Echo Bike Tracker

Privacy Policy — Last updated May 2025

🔒 Echo Bike Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All workout data is stored locally on your device only.

What data the app uses

Echo Bike Tracker accesses the following data during normal use:

  • Bluetooth — to connect to your Rogue Echo Bike v3 and receive live workout metrics (power, cadence, speed). No Bluetooth data leaves your device.
  • Workout data — power, cadence, heart rate, speed, distance, calories, and elapsed time recorded during your sessions.
  • Rider profile — name, weight, age, resting heart rate, and FTP (functional threshold power) that you enter manually.

Where data is stored

All data is stored exclusively on your Mac in:

  • ~/Library/Application Support/Echo Bike Tracker/workouts/

This includes workout session files (.json and .csv), your rider profile, and personal bests. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Bluetooth usage

The app uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) solely to communicate with your Echo Bike's FTMS (Fitness Machine Service) sensor. The connection is device-to-device only. No Bluetooth data is logged, transmitted, or shared beyond your local machine.

Third-party services

Echo Bike Tracker does not use any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or third-party SDKs. There are no in-app purchases and no accounts required.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. No data is collected from anyone — the app works entirely offline.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date.
